# Hacktoberfest Cameroon 2026 — Contribute Track

> **Working list — 24 September 2026**
> Organised by OSSCameroon
> Goal: identify African open-source projects that Hacktoberfest Cameroon participants can contribute to during October 2026.

## How to use this document

This is a prospecting and curation list, not yet the final participant catalogue.

Before a project becomes an official Featured Project — Hacktoberfest Cameroon 2026, OSSCameroon should contact its maintainers and confirm:

- that outside contributions are welcome during October;
- that at least one maintainer can review pull requests;
- that suitable issues can be prepared or labelled;
- the recommended contributor level;
- the preferred contribution workflow;
- the best community/contact channel.

Recommended labels:

`good first issue` · `help wanted` · `documentation` · `frontend` · `backend` · `design` · `data` · `testing` · `intermediate` · `advanced`

## Suggested contribution workflow during Hacktoberfest

1. **Project Discovery** — Participant chooses from the Featured Projects catalogue.
2. **Contributor Onboarding** — Read README, CONTRIBUTING, code of conduct and development setup.
3. **Issue Selection** — Choose an approved issue — participants should not start large unsolicited changes.
4. **Claim / Discuss** — Comment on the issue or follow the workflow preferred by the maintainer.
5. **Build & Test** — Work during the week from home, Atori Space or another hub.
6. **Review** — Submit a focused PR and respond to maintainer feedback.
7. **Track** — Add the issue/PR to the OSSCameroon Hacktoberfest Project Board.
8. **Show & Tell** — Share what was shipped, learned or blocked during the weekly session.

A PR does not have to be merged for the experience to be valuable. Good discussion, testing, documentation, bug reproduction and responding to review feedback are all meaningful open-source work.

---

## Quality rule

Hacktoberfest Cameroon should explicitly discourage:

- mass-generated PRs;
- cosmetic edits made only to increase contribution counts;
- unsolicited large refactors;
- AI-generated changes that the contributor cannot explain;
- opening duplicate issues;
- pressuring maintainers to merge contributions.

The goal is:

> **Useful contributions, respectful collaboration, and long-term participation in open source.**
