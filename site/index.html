Participant tracks

Choose a track — switch anytime

Every participant picks a track and can switch during the month.

Track 01

🛠 Contribute Track

Contribute to existing open-source projects: code, documentation, translation, design, tests, bug reports or community improvements.

Track 02

🚀 Build Track

Build a new open-source project as a team that meets a real need, then publish a first usable version.

Optional

🤖 Open AI Challenge

Build a useful open-source project with open-source AI technology or an open-weight model. AI is never required to take part in the month.

Sprint 1 — First Contribution · 4–9 October

Goal: make a first meaningful contribution in public.

  • Contribute Track — find an issue, talk with the maintainers and open a first contribution.
  • Build Track — form the team, create the repository, write the README and define the first issues.

A contribution can be code, documentation, a translation, a test, a design or a useful bug report.

Sprint 4 — Ship Week · 25–30 October

No new challenge this week. It's for finishing and shipping.

Build Track

  • finish the product;
  • add a README, a licence and CONTRIBUTING.md;
  • improve documentation, tests and CI;
  • ideally, release a v0.1;
  • prepare a 5-minute demo.

Contribute Track

  • finish your pull requests;
  • respond to reviews;
  • fix, test and document;
  • prepare a short write-up of what you learned, even if the contribution isn't merged yet.
31 OCTOBER — SHOW

Cameroon Open Source Festival

Saturday 31 October · Grand finale +

The finale is first and foremost a builders' festival: participants show what they built, contributed and learned during the month. It takes place at a partner venue.

Demo format

  • 5-minute demo;
  • 2 minutes of questions;
  • focus on what was actually built, contributed or learned during the month.

Quality over quantity

Hacktoberfest Cameroon explicitly discourages mass-generated PRs, cosmetic edits made only to raise a contribution count, unsolicited large refactors, AI-generated changes the contributor can't explain, duplicate issues, and pressuring maintainers to merge.

Useful contributions, respectful collaboration, and long-term participation in open source.

During the week

Hack from Home, Hack from Atori

Saturday sets the pace — the work continues all week, wherever you are.

🏠

Hack from Home

Participants can work from home and use the OSSCameroon Telegram group to:

  • find a project or a team;
  • ask for help;
  • share their progress;
  • set up pair programming;
  • prepare their weekly demo.
#Projects#Looking-for-Team#Help#Show-Your-Work#Announcements
📍

Hack from Atori

Throughout October, Atori Space becomes a Hacktoberfest House, with a permanent open-source table, a Contribution Board and a QR code to the OSSCameroon Telegram group.

Open Hack Evenings

Tuesday · 18:00–21:00 Thursday · 18:00–21:00

No talks or workshops: bring your laptop, work, ask for help and meet other contributors. Other hubs can run their own sessions.

Show & Tell — local to each hub

Each presentation lasts about 3 minutes and answers one question:

What did you ship or learn this week?

A rejected PR, a blocker or an important lesson is worth presenting too. Each hub then picks one or two standout contributions, which may be invited to the finale.

Get involved

Join the Telegram community, bring your project into the Contribute Track, or open your hub's doors for an Open Hack Evening.
